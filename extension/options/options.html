Aegis Ad Blocker

Ads, trackers and cookie annoyances — blocked locally in your browser.
No accounts. No cloud. No browsing history ever leaves your machine.

v1.0.0 Manifest V3 · declarativeNetRequest Open source (MIT)

Protection

Master switch. Turning Aegis off disables every list and all cosmetic filtering.

Filter lists

Bundled offline so Aegis works from the very first page load. Lists refresh with each extension update.

My filters

Your own rules in EasyList syntax — one per line. Supported: ||domain^, *, ^, | anchors, $domain=, $script/$image/... , $third-party, $important, @@ exceptions, $generichide.
Currently network rules only; cosmetic rules are ignored.

Paused sites

Protection is suspended on these sites (ads, trackers and cosmetic filtering all allowed).

    None — Aegis is active everywhere.
